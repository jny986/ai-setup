How to use this guide

After installing the skills, name the skill in your request and describe your task. In clients that support slash commands, use /skill-name <task>. Replace <task> with what you want done.

For a specific workflow, add its sub-skill: /git code-review <branch or commit> or /design audit <page or interface>. You can also ask the parent skill to choose a workflow.

Automatic or explicit means the agent may select the skill when your request fits. Explicit request means you must ask for it. Unsure where to start? Ask /what-to-use <task>.
