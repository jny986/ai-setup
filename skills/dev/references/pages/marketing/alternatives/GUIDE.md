# Comparison Page

## Responsibility

A Comparison page helps a buyer evaluate credible alternatives against explicit criteria. It differs from an editorial alternatives article and must not fabricate competitor weaknesses or disguise advertising as neutral research.

## Sections

- **Core:** comparison subject, audience, criteria, evidence, conclusion or fit guidance.
- **Common:** summary table, detailed differences, migration or switching path, proof, FAQ.
- **Conditional:** calculator, methodology, dated evidence, multiple alternatives.

Use current, attributable competitor facts and distinguish measured evidence, official claims, and product judgment. State who each option is best for instead of declaring a universal winner.

## Components And Verification

Comparison Table, Trust Signals, FAQ, CTA Section, and Tabs may apply. Verify source dates, plan and feature accuracy, trademark use, mobile tables, fair criteria, canonical metadata, and links to deeper migration or product pages.
