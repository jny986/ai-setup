# Press And Media Pages

## Shared Responsibility

Press pages serve journalists, analysts, partners, and visitors who need attributable company facts, approved assets, announcements, or third-party coverage.

## Modes

- **Press hub:** provide current company facts, press contact, announcements, approved assets, and routes to coverage or releases.
- **Media kit:** provide downloadable logos, product media, leadership material, usage rules, rights, formats, and asset freshness.
- **Press coverage:** organize attributable third-party mentions without presenting paid placement or copied headlines as independent endorsement.
- **Press release detail:** publish one dated announcement with source identity, factual claims, media contact, assets, and archival state.

## Components And Verification

Hero, Navigation, Grid, List, Card, Logo, Trust Signals, Breadcrumb, Social Share, and downloads may apply. Verify dates, publication source, quote approval, trademarks, rights, file formats, asset resolution, download integrity, contact routing, sponsored-content disclosure, canonical links, metadata, and stale company facts.
