# Case Study Pages

## Shared Responsibility

Case Study pages present attributable customer evidence so a buyer can judge relevance and credibility. They are not anonymous testimonials expanded with invented narrative.

## Index Mode

Support discovery by audience, industry, use case, product, or outcome without reducing every story to an unsupported metric card.

## Detail Mode

Identify the customer and context, prior situation, constraints, implementation, product role, evidence, outcome, limitations, attribution, and a relevant next step. Preserve approval and editing policy.

## Components And Verification

Grid, Card, Hero, Author or Customer identity, Trust Signals, Testimonials, Comparison Table, Social Share, CTA Section, and related stories may apply. Verify publication permission, quote fidelity, metric definition and period, customer identity, sensitive information, links between Index and Detail, metadata, localization approval, and date-sensitive claims.
