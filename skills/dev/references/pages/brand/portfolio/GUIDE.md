# Portfolio Pages

## Shared Responsibility

Portfolio pages help a visitor judge a person's or studio's capabilities through selected work and attributable context. Presentation must not hide the creator's role, collaborators, constraints, or whether a concept was actually shipped.

## Modes

- **Portfolio index:** introduce the practice and organize selected work by capability, sector, medium, or outcome.
- **Project detail:** explain one project's context, role, collaborators, process, artifacts, decisions, outcome, and permitted media.
- **Profile or resume:** present identity, focus, experience, selected credentials, availability, and a suitable contact or hiring path.

## Components And Verification

Hero, Grid, Masonry, Card, Carousel, Author Bio, Tabs, Social Share, and CTA Section may apply. Verify ownership and credits, client permission, shipped versus concept status, outcome attribution, confidential details, media quality and rights, captions, responsive crops, performance, accessible alternatives, contact routing, metadata, and downloadable resume freshness.
