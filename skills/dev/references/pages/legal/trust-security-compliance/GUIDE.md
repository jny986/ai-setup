# Trust, Security, And Compliance Pages

## Shared Responsibility

This family helps prospects, customers, auditors, and procurement teams verify how a product handles security, privacy, compliance, subprocessors, and data processing. It presents approved evidence and current operational facts; it does not invent controls, certifications, legal positions, or guarantees.

## Modes

- **Trust Center:** route reviewers to current security, privacy, compliance, reliability, and evidence resources.
- **Security:** explain security architecture, controls, reporting paths, and shared-responsibility boundaries.
- **Compliance:** identify applicable standards, certification status, scope, dates, and evidence-access process.
- **Privacy Center:** explain data practices, user rights, controls, and routes to approved privacy documents.
- **Subprocessor List:** identify active subprocessors, purpose, location or processing context, and change-notification mechanism.
- **Data Processing:** explain DPA access, data residency, retention, deletion, transfer, and customer-control options from approved sources.

## Boundaries

Legal Policy pages contain binding approved terms. Product Feature pages explain capabilities. Status pages report current service health. This family connects those sources for trust and procurement review without copying their full content or claiming controls that have not been verified.

## Components And Verification

Navigation, Trust Signals, Table of Contents, FAQ, Comparison Table, Task Input or gated evidence request, Announcement Banner, and Footer may apply. Verify every certification, report, region, subprocessor, effective date, contact path, access restriction, download, update mechanism, and cross-link against approved current sources. Do not expose confidential evidence or imply that a certification covers products, regions, or periods outside its stated scope.
