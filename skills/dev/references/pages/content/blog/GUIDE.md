# Blog Index

## Responsibility

A blog index helps readers understand a publication's scope, discover a useful article, and continue browsing. It is not an undifferentiated card grid or the article template itself.

## Page Contract

Use the real post collection, publication identity, taxonomy, dates, authors, imagery, pagination, and routes. Decide whether discovery is chronological, editorial, topical, search-led, or a deliberate combination.

## Sections

- **Core:** publication identity and article discovery.
- **Common:** featured or latest content, article collection, pagination or continuation.
- **Conditional:** categories, tags, authors, search, newsletter, editorial series.

## Product Adaptation

Publications and news products emphasize editorial hierarchy and recency. SaaS blogs may emphasize educational topics and product-adjacent journeys. Creator businesses may emphasize the author, series, and subscription relationship.

## Components

The Bricks Blog Index pattern can guide composition. Card, Grid, navigation, newsletter, and announcements remain subordinate to content discovery.

## Verify

Test no posts, one post, large archives, missing images, long titles, disabled taxonomy, pagination, keyboard links, canonical and index metadata, article destinations, and duplicate featured/latest presentation.
