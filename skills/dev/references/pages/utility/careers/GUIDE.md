# Careers Pages

## Shared Responsibility

Careers pages let candidates judge the employer and available work, while job details let them understand and apply for one real role. They must not invent culture, benefits, salary, location flexibility, or hiring commitments.

## Modes

- **Careers hub:** present the employer proposition, working model, hiring process, verified benefits, and discoverable open roles.
- **Job detail:** state role status, responsibilities, requirements, location, employment type, compensation where supplied or required, process, accommodations, and a functioning application path.

## Components And Verification

Hero, Grid or List, Card, Task Input, Tabs, FAQ, Testimonials, Trust Signals, and CTA Section may apply. Verify role freshness, ATS synchronization, filters, closed-role behavior, legal disclosures, salary and location accuracy, application consent, upload behavior, confirmation, accommodations, structured data, and localization.
