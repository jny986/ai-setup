# Feedback And Disclosure Pages

## Shared Responsibility

Feedback pages collect a specific response through a clear and safe process. Disclosure pages make a material relationship, sponsorship, affiliate interest, methodology, or relevant limitation understandable where readers need it.

## Modes

- **Feedback:** identify subject and purpose, collect the minimum useful response, explain handling, and provide success, failure, and follow-up states.
- **Disclosure:** state the relevant relationship or limitation in plain language, scope it to affected content or actions, and provide effective dates and contact or policy links when needed.

## Boundaries

Feedback is not a substitute for support, incident reporting, appeals, or legally required complaint handling. A Disclosure is not a generic Legal Policy and should also appear contextually near the affected claim, link, review, or promotion when necessary.

## Components And Verification

Task Input or Form, Modal, FAQ, Announcement Banner, Breadcrumb, and Contact path may apply. Verify consent, privacy, moderation, abuse and sensitive data handling, receipts, routing, accessibility, effective dates, plain-language visibility, contextual placement, legal review requirements, metadata, and indexing policy.
