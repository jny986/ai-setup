# Call to Action Section

## Scope

A call to action section concentrates the context, evidence, and next step needed at a decision point. CTA is otherwise a semantic role that may be carried by a link, button, or form action; this reference covers the complete section only.

## Decisions

Choose the action, destination, commitment level, supporting proof, placement, and whether a secondary path is genuinely needed. Match the section to the page stage instead of repeating the same conversion request everywhere.

## Constraints

- Use button semantics for actions and links for navigation.
- Labels describe the result rather than relying on vague text.
- Do not promise access, pricing, or outcomes the destination cannot deliver.
- Keep one clear primary action within the section.

## Acceptance criteria

The primary action reaches its stated destination or result. Loading, failure, keyboard focus, analytics ownership, mobile layout, and long translations agree with the surrounding offer and claims.
