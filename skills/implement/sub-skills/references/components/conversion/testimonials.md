# Testimonials

## Scope

A testimonials component presents attributable customer evidence relevant to the decision being made. It is proof, not decorative praise.

## Decisions

Select claims by audience and page context. Define attribution, permission, source, date sensitivity, editing policy, and whether the evidence is a quote, review, result, or case-study excerpt.

## Constraints

- Do not invent, merge, or materially rewrite quotations.
- Distinguish customer statements from verified performance data.
- Use real identities and logos only with appropriate permission.
- Avoid carousels when they hide evidence or make reading harder.

## Acceptance criteria

Source records support the quoted text, attribution links, and consent. Long quotations, missing portraits or logos, mobile reading, and any carousel controls preserve access to the evidence.
