# Homepage

## Scope

A homepage identifies the product or organization, establishes its primary value, and routes mixed first-time and returning visitors toward the next meaningful destination. It is not a campaign landing page with one traffic source or a directory of every available feature.

## Contract

The page must make the identity, audience, offer, proof, and principal paths understandable without requiring prior product knowledge. Preserve stable global navigation and use real product evidence.

## Content

- **Core:** identity and value entry; primary paths; global continuation.
- **Common:** product evidence, major capabilities or categories, trust, representative use cases.
- **Conditional:** real tool input, customer stories, latest content, newsletter, FAQ.

Section order follows the audience's recognition and evaluation needs. These visible regions need not map one-to-one to source components.

## Product Adaptation

- **SaaS:** clarify product, buyer, outcome, proof, and route to product, pricing, or signup.
- **AI tool:** expose the real input or a faithful product demonstration when immediate use is central.
- **Ecommerce:** prioritize category and product discovery, promotion, trust, and fulfillment expectations.
- **Publication:** prioritize editorial identity, current coverage, sections, and subscription.
- **Portfolio:** prioritize person or studio identity, selected work, role, and contact.

## Composition

Navigation and footer establish the page boundary. Optional regions depend on evidence and the visitor’s task; visible sections need not match code component boundaries.
