# FAQ Page

## Scope

A FAQ page provides a maintained destination for recurring questions that span the product or customer journey. It is distinct from a short FAQ Section embedded to resolve objections on another page.

## Content

- **Core:** real questions, direct answers, meaningful grouping, navigation or search when volume requires it.
- **Common:** product, account, billing, privacy, troubleshooting, and contact escalation.
- **Conditional:** anchors, search, audience filters, related documentation, update ownership.

Questions must come from support, sales, product, search, or confirmed user input. Do not manufacture keyword questions or duplicate legal and documentation text; summarize and link to the maintained source.

## Component candidates

- **Primary candidates:** [FAQ Section](../../components/content/faq-section.md)
- **Common:** [Navigation Menu](../../components/navigation/navigation-menu.md)
- **Conditional:** [Tabs](../../components/content/tabs.md), [Accordion](../../components/content/accordion.md), [Task Input](../../components/utility/task-input.md), [Call to Action Section](../../components/conversion/cta.md)

## Acceptance coverage

Answer accuracy, direct links, crawlable content, keyboard disclosure, duplicate questions, structured data consistency when present, and escalation paths.
