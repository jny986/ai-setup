# Publication And News Pages

## Scope

Publication pages help readers discover, follow, and assess timely editorial work. They must preserve authorship, publication and update time, corrections, source distinctions, and editorial context.

## Modes

- **Publication home:** express current editorial priorities, lead stories, sections, formats, and subscription paths without becoming an arbitrary card wall.
- **Topic page:** maintain a coherent subject archive with useful context, chronology, and related coverage.
- **News article:** present one reported item with headline, dek, author, dates, body, evidence, corrections, related coverage, and appropriate sharing or subscription paths.
- **Live coverage:** append attributable timestamped updates, preserve chronology, distinguish confirmed facts from developing information, and expose current status.
- **Author page:** identify one contributor, role, expertise, disclosures, biography, and attributable work.
