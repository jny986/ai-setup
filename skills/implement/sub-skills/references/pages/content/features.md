# Product And Capability Pages

## Scope

Product and capability pages help a buyer understand what is offered, what it can do, how the parts relate, and whether a specific capability fits the buyer's work. They must use real product behavior and evidence, not a renamed list of generic benefits.

## Modes

- **Product hub:** orient across products, editions, or major product areas and route visitors to the right detail page.
- **Product detail:** explain one product or independently evaluated offer, including audience, workflow, boundaries, proof, commercial path, and next step.
- **Feature hub:** organize capabilities by task or product area rather than presenting an undifferentiated grid.
- **Feature detail:** demonstrate one capability through the user's problem, interaction, output, limits, dependencies, and related capabilities.

## Boundaries

A Product page describes an offer a customer can adopt. A Feature page describes a capability within an offer. Use Case pages start from a scenario; Solution pages start from a segment or business outcome. Do not create separate pages where the available evidence cannot support a distinct decision.
