# 404 Page

## Scope

A 404 page accurately reports that the requested resource does not exist and gives users a small number of useful recovery paths. It must not turn every unknown URL into a successful marketing page or redirect users without a true replacement.

## Contract

Return the platform's real not-found status, preserve enough product identity for orientation, and offer recovery based on the current surface. Redirect only when an equivalent destination is known.

## Content

- **Core:** clear not-found message and recovery action.
- **Common:** home or previous destination, primary navigation, search where useful.
- **Conditional:** popular destinations, contextual help, report-link path, authenticated app return.

## Product Adaptation

Public sites may offer home, categories, search, or current content. Ecommerce may offer category or product discovery. Publications may offer search and recent coverage. Web apps should return users to a safe authenticated destination without leaking unavailable object information.

## Composition

Recovery actions take priority over promotional evidence or interruption.

## Component candidates

- **Common:** [Navigation Menu](../../components/navigation/navigation-menu.md), [Call to Action Section](../../components/conversion/cta.md)
- **Conditional:** [Task Input](../../components/utility/task-input.md), [Card](../../components/layout/card.md), [Footer](../../components/navigation/footer.md)
- **Discouraged:** [Modal](../../components/conversion/popup.md), [Carousel](../../components/layout/carousel.md), [Testimonials](../../components/conversion/testimonials.md)

## Acceptance coverage

The actual HTTP or framework not-found behavior, arbitrary unknown paths, deleted content, authenticated and unauthenticated states, localization, keyboard operation, mobile layout, recovery links, analytics without sensitive URLs, and absence of soft-404 behavior.
