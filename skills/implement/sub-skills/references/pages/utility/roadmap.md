# Roadmap Page

## Scope

A public Roadmap page communicates selected future direction and stage without exposing internal plans or converting exploration into contractual delivery promises. It is distinct from feedback intake and from the shipped Changelog.

## Content

- **Core:** scope, status model, roadmap items, expectation disclaimer, update ownership.
- **Common:** planned, in progress, shipped or recently shipped; feedback path.
- **Conditional:** filters, voting, item detail, notifications, request submission.

Use approved roadmap items and states. A lightweight data source can supply the display; voting, deduplication, notifications, customer identity, and planning behavior depend on supporting systems owned by the coordinating feature.
