# Marketplace And Directory Pages

## Scope

Marketplace and directory pages help users discover, compare, trust, and act on third-party or multi-provider listings. They must distinguish platform assertions, provider-supplied information, reviews, sponsorship, and transactional responsibility.

## Modes

- **Marketplace home:** orient users across categories, demand and supply actions, trust model, and current inventory.
- **Directory index:** provide systematic discovery across categories, locations, attributes, or other maintained taxonomy.
- **Listing detail:** identify one provider, item, place, or resource with attributable facts, freshness, evidence, availability, and a valid action path.
- **Submission:** collect a new or claimed listing, explain eligibility, evidence, review, fees, publication state, and update ownership.

## Boundaries

Use Commerce pages when the site is the merchant and owns checkout. Use Marketplace pages when multiple sellers or providers participate. A Directory may only route or inform; it must not imply verification, availability, or endorsement that does not exist.
