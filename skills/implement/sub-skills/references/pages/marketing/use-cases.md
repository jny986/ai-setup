# Use Case Pages

## Scope

Use Case pages connect a specific role, situation, workflow, or business goal to product capabilities and believable outcomes. They are distinct from Feature pages, broad industry Solutions, and educational Blog articles.

## Index Mode

Help visitors self-select by a coherent dimension and reach a relevant detail page. Avoid mixing personas, industries, tasks and company sizes without clear navigation.

## Detail Mode

Establish the audience and job, current problem, workflow, product mechanism, evidence, limitations, and next action. Use the audience's real language and approved proof.

## Acceptance coverage

Dimension consistency, real product capability, evidence, links between Index and Detail, overlapping pages, canonical metadata, localization, and avoidance of generic copy that could fit any audience.
