# Program Pages

## Scope

Program pages explain a structured relationship, its value exchange, eligibility, obligations, application or enrollment, and what happens after submission. Startup, student, creator, affiliate, referral, and partner programs retain separate identities even when they share this model.

## Variants

- **Startup:** company eligibility, stage, benefits, duration and verification.
- **Student:** academic verification, regional availability, duration and renewal.
- **Creator:** creative fit, access, amplification, deliverables and rights.
- **Affiliate:** commission, attribution, payout, prohibited promotion and disclosure.
- **Referral:** inviter and invitee rewards, qualification, limits and abuse prevention.
- **Partner:** partner type, capability, commercial relationship, certification or listing.

## Content

Core regions cover audience, benefits, eligibility, obligations, process, review, terms, and response expectation.
