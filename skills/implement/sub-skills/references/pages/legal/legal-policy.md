# Legal Policy Page

## Scope

A Legal Policy page presents one approved policy or agreement with stable identity, effective dates, readable structure, and access from relevant product surfaces. This reference covers structure and rendering; approved source documents own legal terms.

## Modes

Privacy, Terms, Cookie, Acceptable Use, Refund, Shipping, Disclosure, and other policies may share one page model while retaining separate routes and approved source documents.

## Content

- **Core:** policy identity, operator, effective or updated date, complete approved text, contact path.
- **Common:** table of contents, jurisdiction or scope, change notice, related policies.
- **Conditional:** consent controls, regional variants, downloadable copy, version history.

## Component candidates

- **Common:** [Table of Contents](../../components/navigation/toc.md), [Breadcrumb](../../components/navigation/breadcrumb.md), [Footer](../../components/navigation/footer.md)
- **Conditional:** [Navigation Menu](../../components/navigation/navigation-menu.md)

## Acceptance coverage

Approved text source, jurisdiction and market variants, dates, anchors, printable reading, consent links, localization review, canonical URL, indexing policy, and footer discovery. Flag content for human legal review when approval is absent.
